这张表怎么用

这两张表（昆雅→英语、英语→昆雅）覆盖本课程第 2–20 课练习中出现的全部词汇，少数专有名词除外。只出现在课文正文、未进入练习的词不收；第 1 课练习用词也不收，因为那些词对做题没有意义。注意：所有词都以最基本形式列出，练习原文里往往用的是带屈折词尾或派生形式；昆雅→英语表不含屈折词尾，英语→昆雅表则收了人称词尾。用法建议：做某课练习前先在该课「课末词汇」里记住 12 个新词，再按英文或昆雅词反查即可。

原著词汇表篇首说明
QUENYA VOCABULARY
These lists, Quenya-English and English-Quenya, cover the vocabulary used in the exercises of Lessons 2-20 of this course, except for a few proper names. (Words that occur in the main text of the lessons, but that are not used in the exercises, are excluded. The words occurring in the exercises of Lesson One are also excluded, since their meanings are irrelevant for the exercises.) Notice that all words are here listed in their most basic form; the actual text of the exercises would often employ or require more complex inflected or derivative forms. No inflectional endings are included in the Quenya-English list, but pronominal endings are included in the English-Quenya list below. – For more extensive Quenya wordlists, see www.Ardalambion.com/wordlists.htm
